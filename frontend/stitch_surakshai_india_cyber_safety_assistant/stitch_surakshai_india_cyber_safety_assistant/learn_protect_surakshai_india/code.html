Learn the signs before you lose money

धोखाधड़ी से बचें (Avoid fraud). Scammers are getting smarter, but so can you. Understanding how modern digital scams work is your first line of defense.

Bank Alerts

dangerous Scam Attempt

Common Scams in India

Learn to recognize the red flags before responding to any unexpected message or call.

timer

False Urgency

Scammers create panic (account blocking, electricity disconnection) to make you act quickly without thinking.

Example Message expand_more
"Dear consumer, your electricity power will be disconnected at 9:30 PM today from electricity office because your previous month bill was not updated."
verified

What safe companies do:

They send formal notices with specific dates, usually days or weeks in advance, and address you by name.

psychology

Practical Habit:

Pause for 60 seconds. Do not click links. Check official apps directly.

currency_rupee

UPI PIN Trick

Fraudsters claim they are sending you money, but ask you to enter your UPI PIN to "receive" it.

Example Scenario expand_more
"I sent Rs. 5000 by mistake. Please return it. I am initiating a payment request on your GPay. Just enter your PIN to process the refund."
verified

What safe companies do:

Money sent to you is deposited automatically. You NEVER need a PIN to receive money.

psychology

Practical Habit:

PIN = पैसे कटेंगे। (PIN means money gets deducted).

work

Task-Based Fake Jobs

Offers of easy money for "liking YouTube videos" or "writing reviews", ending in demands for "investment".

Example Message expand_more
"Earn ₹3000-₹5000 daily working from home! Just like 5 videos a day. WhatsApp us on this number to start immediately."
verified

What safe companies do:

Legitimate employers conduct interviews and never ask you to pay money to get paid.

psychology

Practical Habit:

If it sounds too good to be true, it is a scam. Block and report the number.
